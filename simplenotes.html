What it does

Native .enex support

Open and save Evernote-format files directly. No conversion step, no proprietary database, no migration cost if you decide to leave.

Full-text search

Find any note in milliseconds across thousands. Supports AND, OR, NOT, exact phrases, prefix matching, and a fuzzy mode for typos.

Rich text editor

Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, font sizes, colors, highlights, bullets, numbered lists, checklists, and tables.

AES encryption

Protect sensitive sections with an optional password. Encrypted text is sealed inside the .enex file and only decrypted when you provide the password.

PDF export

Export any note to PDF with formatting, colors, embedded images, and tables preserved.

Attachments

Attach images, PDFs, and other files to your notes. Images embed inline in the note view.

Tags & NoteFolders

Organize how you like. Filter by tag, by NoteFolder, by date range, or by whether a note has attachments.

Optional Drive sharing

Share a NoteFolder with another user via your own Google Drive account. Only the folders you select are uploaded — everything else stays local.

Your notes don't leave your Mac.

No account. No analytics. No telemetry. No advertising identifiers. The optional Google Drive sharing uploads only the folders you explicitly choose, to your own Drive — the developer never sees them.
