SimpleNotes — Privacy Policy

Overview

SimpleNotes is a local-first note management application. Your notes are stored on your device and are never sent to our servers. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

Data Storage

All note content, search indexes, and application preferences are stored locally on your device:

  • Notes: stored as .enex files in the folder you choose to open.
  • Search index: a SQLite database stored alongside your .enex files.
  • Preferences: stored in your local application support directory on your device.

We have no access to any of this data. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Google Drive sharing feature described below.

Google Drive Integration (Optional)

SimpleNotes offers an optional Google Drive integration that lets you share NoteFolders with other users. If you choose to connect a Google Drive account:

  • Authentication: SimpleNotes uses Google's OAuth 2.0 to authenticate. Your Google password is never seen or stored by SimpleNotes. An access token is stored locally on your device.
  • Data transmitted: When you share a NoteFolder to Drive, the note content in that folder is uploaded to your personal Google Drive storage. Only the NoteFolders you explicitly choose to share are uploaded.
  • Scope: SimpleNotes requests the drive.file scope, which limits access to only files and folders created by SimpleNotes. It cannot read or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Lock files: When editing a shared note, a small lock file containing your system username and computer name is temporarily stored on Google Drive to prevent editing conflicts. The lock file is removed when you finish editing.

You can disconnect your Google Drive account at any time from NoteFolder > Manage Drive Connections. Disconnecting removes the local access token. Files already uploaded to Google Drive remain in your Drive and can be deleted from there directly.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No analytics or usage telemetry
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contact information
  • No browsing or search history
  • No device identifiers sent to external servers

Encryption

SimpleNotes includes an optional note encryption feature that uses AES with a password you provide. Encrypted content is stored locally and, if shared via Google Drive, is transmitted in its encrypted form. We do not have access to your encryption passwords and cannot decrypt your content. If you forget your password, the encrypted content cannot be recovered.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service SimpleNotes communicates with is Google Drive, and only when you explicitly enable the integration. No other third-party services, SDKs, or analytics frameworks are included in the application.

Children's Privacy

SimpleNotes does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Since the app does not collect data from any users, no special provisions are necessary.

Changes to This Policy
