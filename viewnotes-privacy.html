ViewNotes

Privacy Policy

ViewNotes opens the encrypted note backups created by SimpleNotes and stored in your own Google Drive. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

  • Last updated: August 31, 2026
  • iPhone app
In short: ViewNotes has no servers of its own. It collects nothing, sends nothing to us, and never sees your master password. Your notes are decrypted only on your iPhone.

01 No data collection

ViewNotes has no backend infrastructure. We do not collect, store, receive, or transmit your personal information, your notes, or your usage to us or to any third party we control. There is no analytics SDK and no advertising in the app.

02 On-device decryption

Your notes are encrypted. ViewNotes downloads the encrypted backups from your Google Drive and decrypts them locally on your iPhone, using the master password you set in SimpleNotes. Your master password is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere off your device. Because we never receive it, we cannot recover or reset it — that is managed in SimpleNotes on your Mac.

03 Google account access

When you sign in with Google, ViewNotes requests only the drive.file permission. This limits it to the single Drive folder you select — not the rest of your Drive. ViewNotes reads your encrypted backups from that folder and, when you choose to send a Temp Note, writes that one file back to it.

All access is to your own Google account. Your interaction with Google is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. ViewNotes uses the Google Sign-In SDK to perform authentication; any data that SDK processes is handled per Google’s disclosures and is not sent to us.

04 Microphone and speech

If you dictate a Temp Note, transcription is performed on-device. Audio and transcripts are not sent to us or to any third-party recognition service.

05 On-device storage

Decrypted notes may be cached on your device to enable offline reading and search. This cache is protected by iOS file protection and is removed when you sign out or choose Forget master password. Notes are kept out of the system (Spotlight) search index unless you explicitly enable that option in Settings.

06 Children

ViewNotes is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
