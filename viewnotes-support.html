01 Getting started

ViewNotes is the read side of SimpleNotes on your phone. It needs SimpleNotes on your Mac, already backing up to Google Drive.

Do I need SimpleNotes on a Mac to use ViewNotes?

Yes. ViewNotes is a companion viewer — it opens the encrypted backups that SimpleNotes writes to your Google Drive. On a brand-new account with no backups yet, there’s nothing for it to show. Set up SimpleNotes to back up to Drive first, then sign in here with the same Google account.

How do I sign in?

Tap Sign In with Google on the first screen and choose the account that holds your backups. ViewNotes asks Google for only the narrow drive.file permission — it can never see the rest of your Drive, just the one folder you hand it next.

How do I choose my Backups folder?

After signing in you’ll see One more step. Tap Choose Backups Folder, then use Google’s own picker to select the folder SimpleNotes writes to (usually named SimpleNotes Backups). That grant covers everything inside that folder, so ViewNotes can list each backup it finds. Picked the wrong one? Change it any time from the ••• menu → Change Folder.

What do the lock icons mean?

Each backup in the list shows its state:

  • Locked — not yet unlocked this session. Tap it and enter your master password to open it.
  • Unlocked — decrypted and ready to read. Swipe left → Lock to re-lock it and clear its decrypted contents from memory.

02 Unlocking & your master password

Your notes are decrypted on your iPhone using the master password you chose in SimpleNotes. That password is never uploaded — which is also why it can’t be recovered here.

How do I unlock a backup?

Tap a locked backup and enter your master password — the same one you set in SimpleNotes on your Mac. ViewNotes decrypts the backup on your device and opens it for reading.

I forgot my master password.

ViewNotes can’t recover or reset it. Your master password is never stored on your device or uploaded anywhere, so there’s nothing for ViewNotes to look up. Your password lives with SimpleNotes on your Mac — recover or change it there. If you change it in SimpleNotes, use the new password the next time you unlock in ViewNotes.

A note has a locked block inside it. How do I read it?

Individual encrypted blocks inside a note have their own password. Tap the block and enter that block password to decrypt just that section — on your device, like everything else.

What do “Remember master password” and “Forget master password” do?

Remember (in Settings) caches your folder’s data key in the iPhone Keychain so background sync can keep offline copies current without re-prompting. Turn it off and that cached key is wiped — background sync then can’t run until you unlock again.

Forget master password wipes every cached key and the decrypted on-device copies of your notes, returning ViewNotes to a fully locked state.

03 Reading, search & offline

Read your notes with their original formatting, search across everything you’ve opened, and keep folders available without a connection.

How does search work?

The magnifying glass (top-left of the backup list) searches across every backup you’ve opened — both note titles and body text. A backup you’ve never unlocked isn’t in the index yet; open it once and it becomes searchable.

Can I read my notes offline?

Yes. Turn on Keep notes offline and ViewNotes downloads a folder’s notes so you can read and search them without a connection. When the list is being served from saved copies, a yellow “Offline — showing saved copies” banner appears at the top.

Will my notes show up in the iPhone’s Spotlight search?

Only if you choose to. Notes are kept out of the system search index by default. You can opt in from Settings if you want notes to appear in Spotlight — the setting explains the trade-off before you turn it on.

04 Sending a Temp Note

ViewNotes is a viewer — it never changes your real notes. The one thing it writes is a Temp Note you deliberately compose and send to your Mac.

What is a Temp Note?

Tap the compose icon (the pencil-in-square, top-right) to write a quick note on your phone and send it up to Drive. SimpleNotes on your Mac imports it later. It’s a one-way path from phone to Mac — it doesn’t edit or overwrite any existing note.

Can I dictate instead of typing?

Yes. The Temp Note editor supports dictation, and transcription happens entirely on your iPhone — the audio and the transcript never leave the device. You can also attach photos to a Temp Note.

05 Privacy & security

ViewNotes has no servers of its own. It collects nothing, and it never sees your master password.

Can you (the developer) see my notes?

No. There is no ViewNotes backend. Your encrypted backups live in your Google Drive, and they’re decrypted only on your iPhone with your master password. We collect no data and there is no analytics or advertising in the app.

Why does ViewNotes use Google sign-in?

Because that’s where your backups are — SimpleNotes stores them in your Google Drive. ViewNotes signs in to read them, requesting only the drive.file scope, which limits it to the single folder you select. Google (and anyone else looking at your Drive) sees only encrypted bytes.

Is my master password ever uploaded?

Never. It isn’t transmitted and isn’t stored off your device. All decryption is done locally on your iPhone.

06 Troubleshooting

The quick fixes for the issues people hit most.

Sign-in fails, or ViewNotes can’t reach Drive

  • Check your internet connection, then try Sign In with Google again.
  • Make sure you’re choosing the Google account that actually holds your SimpleNotes backups.
  • If sign-in was interrupted, open the ••• menu → Sign Out, then sign in again cleanly.

The backup list is empty, or shows the wrong folders

You may have selected the wrong Drive folder during setup. Open the ••• menu → Change Folder and pick the folder SimpleNotes backs up to (usually SimpleNotes Backups). Pull down on the list to refresh from Drive.

My master password isn’t accepted

Use the exact master password you set in SimpleNotes — it’s case-sensitive. If you changed it in SimpleNotes recently, use the new one. ViewNotes can’t reset it (see Unlocking & your password).

Search isn’t finding a note I know exists

Search covers backups you’ve already opened. Unlock the backup that contains the note once, and it’ll be included from then on.

I want to fully remove my data from this iPhone

Settings → Forget master password wipes the cached keys and the decrypted on-device copies. Signing out and deleting the app removes everything ViewNotes stored locally. Your backups in Google Drive are untouched — ViewNotes never deletes them.
